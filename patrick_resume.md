# Profile

I build products that help experts make better decisions.

For more than a decade I've been drawn toward problems where important work is slowed down by fragmented systems, repetitive processes, or tools that people tolerate rather than enjoy using. Whether automating accounting workflows, redesigning executive reporting, building investment analytics platforms, or introducing AI into investment teams, my approach has remained remarkably consistent: understand how people actually work, identify the friction they've accepted as normal, and build products that make better ways of working feel obvious.

I enjoy operating where product thinking, AI, analytics, and business strategy meet. My favorite projects start with a simple question:

**What if there were a better way?**

From there I work closely with stakeholders to understand how decisions are made, rapidly prototype ideas, iterate through feedback, and build products that people genuinely want to use.
