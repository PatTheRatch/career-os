## Profile

Looking back, I've been doing the same job my whole career. Only the titles changed.

I started in audit, teaching myself to automate the reconciliations nobody wanted to touch. Today I design AI-powered decision systems, internal AI products, and MCP-based tools used daily by investment teams across a $1.4T global asset manager. Accounting, finance, investment risk, artificial intelligence: just the tools I happened to be holding at each stage. The actual work never changed: find the friction people have quietly accepted as normal, and design it away.

For most of my career my edge was writing better Python, SQL, and dashboards than the people around me. AI is dissolving that edge. It moves the value to the part I cared about all along: knowing which problem is worth solving before anyone writes a line of code.

Every project I care about still starts with the same three words: *what if...*
